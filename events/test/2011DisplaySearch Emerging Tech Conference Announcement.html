Emerging Display Technologies Conference
Returns to San Jose, California
Now in its third year, the DisplaySearch Emerging Display Technologies Conference is moving to a two-day format in 2011, enabling deeper coverage of more topics.

The conference covers the hottest new display technologies, including touch screens, 3D displays, and OLED displays. It is a forum for discovering new display technologies and discussing how they can create growth.

Agenda highlights include:

Tuesday, August 16, 2011 - Expanding the Definition of Flat Panel Displays

Touch Screens

  • Multi-touch, new technologies, and other solutions supporting this market that is forecast to reach $14 billion in revenue by 2016
  • New technologies bring opportunities to reach commercialization

3D Displays

  • Challenges and opportunities for 3D in display applications including desktop monitors, mobile PCs, gaming, mobile handsets, and digital cameras and camcorders

Wednesday, August 17, 2011 - Innovating Around the Mainstream

Efficient/Low Power Technologies for Mobile Devices

  • New technologies, including reflective technologies and embedded projectors, need to move up the performance curve faster than TFT LCD moves down the power consumption and cost curves
  • How can low power technologies win?

OLEDs and LEDs for Displays and Solid State Lighting

  • Significant AMOLED capacity expansions are expected among Asian panel makers
  • OLED lighting, which has as many differences as similarities with displays, is coming to the market
  • New materials and technologies are being developed to improve the efficiency of LEDs for display backlighting and general illumination
