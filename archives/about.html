HDTV MAGAZINE IS THE FIRST PUBLICATION ON THE INTERNET OR IN PAPER DEVOTED TO THE CONSUMERS'' AWARENESS AND KNOWLEDGE OF HDTV.

We are an independent source of objective and qualified subjective information about the technology and the business decisions which drive it.

Our history dates back to 1984 when our founder, Dale Cripps, first became aware of HDTV. He recognized that it was a highly worthwhile product and that it would would have hurdle after hurdle to surmount and that the movement could, without proper encouragement and nourishment, wear the movement down.

From that recognition we founded the HDTV Newsletter, which became the bible of the industry read around the world. Not only did our company publish it for 15 years we also sponsored and co-sponsored some of the most comprehensive conferences on the subject ever held.

The standard was set in the United States by FCC decree in early 1997, and on November 16, 1998, the industry was launched with official ceremonies in Washington, DC. HDTV Magazine was born on that day in Washington and has published daily ever since.

We were the first to publish a daily HDTV programming guide which was designed to give a daily reminder to all who were contemplating acquiring HDTV that programming from major sources was available and to keep in daily touch with those people who had grown in to enthusiasts. The chief mission of HDTV Magazine is to lead the industry past any difficult shoals and to set forth a series of events intended to inspire the artist of our times to create productions fit for the medium and for a global audience.
