The IAB Announces Two Major Events:

1. UNDERSTANDING METADATA at IAB Campus, Montreux, 26 - 28 February 2001.
Since August 1998, when the EBU/SMPTE Task Force published its report, Metadata has become one of the most important and the most fiercely debated topics. Its importance is undeniable, but do we all really understand why Metadata is needed, where it is needed, and what impact it will have? Do we know what is the potential it offers? How will we generate, distribute and use Metadata?

The International Academy of Broadcasting, responding to the perceived need, and in consultation with experts in the field, has invited Leading European experts as speakers who will help participants to acquire the necessary knowledge and to be fully acquainted with Metadata.

The advance programme, registration and hotel booking forms can be retrieved from http://www.iab.ch.


2. DVB WORLD 2001 Conference at Alexander Hotel, Dublin, 7 - 9 March, 2001.
The International Academy of Broadcasting (IAB) and the DVB Project, in association with the Technical University of Braunschweig (TUB), Digital TV Group, Pro-Mpeg Forum and Digitag would like to invite you to take part at the "DVB World 2001" Conference in Dublin from 7-9 March 2001.

This Conference will feature as speakers and panellist leading developers and implementers of digital broadcasting and will be certainly the most important event in this area in 2001.

The Advance programme, registration and hotel booking forms can be retrieved from http://www.iab.ch or http://www.dvb.org. Official Publication by TVBEurope.
