Monday, June 25, 2001

Spirit DataCine Brings Unparalleled Film Image Quality To Top Chilean and Argentinean Finishing and Post Production Facility

Thomson multimedia Broadcast Solutions, a division of Thomson multimedia has announced the sale of two Spirit DataCine film scanners to Chilefilms Group, a leading Latin- American Holding Company in the fields of production, post-production and film laboratory services with facilities in Chile, Argentina, Brazil and Mexico.


June 22 2001 - The Spirit DataCine film scanner is a joint development between Thomson multimedia and Eastman Kodak. The combination of the Advanced Imaging Head from Eastman Kodak and Thomson's precision transport mechanisms and high-speed digital signal processing has resulted in a new benchmark for high-quality film transfers. This has been acknowledged by over 200 users world wide of the Spirit DataCine. The new standard applies to standard definition, high definition and high-speed data scanning.

Chilefilms Group will install one DataCine at Chilefilms, their laboratory and post-production facility located in Santiago, Chile and a second DataCine at their laboratory and post-production house Videocolor, in Buenos Aires, Argentina. Each DataCine will be equipped with Da Vinci's 2K colour corrector, Aaton's KeyLink V8 Code Reader and Thomson's VS4 advanced film grain & noise reducer and dirt & scratch concealing system.

This state-of-the-art equipment will position Chilefilms and Videocolor as the leading post-production facility in both countries, and among the most advanced in the world, offering the best available technology for video and data transfer. In acquiring the Spirit DataCine, Chilefilms and Videocolor have prepared their path to a future entry into the world of high definition post-production, high-resolution digital mastering and film restoration.

In making the announcement, Sundeep Jinsi, General Manager, South American NSO, Thomson multimedia Broadcast Solutions, commented: "We are pleased that Chilefilms Group have chosen the Spirit DataCine. Today's requirements for film scanning are diverse and technically demanding, requirements the Spirit easily meets. Chilefilms Group enjoys a long-term reputation as having the largest of Latin America's top production, post-production and film laboratory services facilities in the region. We are proud to be a part of their continued success."

"Thomson has over ten years of research in the Spirit DataCine, and has done a phenomenal job of marketing their system and building confidence in the brand", commented Cristin Varela, CEO, Chilefilms Group. "Our clients are very technically savvy and we are bringing in the Spirit in direct response to the market requirements. To improve our leadership position, we must continue to invest in innovative technologies and be ahead of our competitors. We have the strongest Telecine talent in the region at Chilefilms and Videocolor and our artists are looking forward to working with the Spirit."
