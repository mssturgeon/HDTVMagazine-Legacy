People speak of the real world, the practical world as if it were somehow more sensible than the ideal world, the spiritual universe.

In fact, most of the delusional thinking comes from underestimating the practical importance of the dreamer's visions, the poet's thoughts, the mystic's prayers.

The invisible world is always at work. Tune in to that and so-called reality is revealed as having huge holes, great galloping gaps, and flaws that boggle the mind. What people take for granted in the sleep of ordinary consciousness is astonishing.

So if you awaken yourself and begin to dream consciously you are creating a new reality.

That is how beauty enters the world.

There was a study a few years ago that claimed that the most productive people in any office spent 40% of their time staring out the window.

That's practical.
