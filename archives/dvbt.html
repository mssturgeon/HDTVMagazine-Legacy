DVB-T

    DVB-T is defined for a 6MHz channel (as is the Japanese ISDB-T system). DVB-T is one standard with many different options, all of which can be received by chips on the market now, such as the LSI L64780. The data capacities of all of these options in 6MHz, 7MHz, and 8MHz channels are all known.


    A DVB-T standard for the USA could be written in a few weeks, based on the DVB-T specification and work already done in Australia. While that was being done, any extra interference tests required could also be carried out (in fact they already have been done in Brazil). Integrated circuits are already available from LSI and several other IC manufacturers, and most TV manufacturers already have designs for incorporating them in 7 and 8MHz receivers - 6MHz receivers shouldn't take more than 6 months.
