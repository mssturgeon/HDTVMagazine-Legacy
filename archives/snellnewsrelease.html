Thursday, April 5, 2001

New Madison Avenue Office Extends Snell & Wilcox Presence in Eastern U.S. and Canada

NEW YORK CITY, April 5, 2001 - As a major part of its recent realignment and expansion of its Americas operation, Snell & Wilcox Incorporated announces the opening of a new office in mid-town Manhattan that more than doubles the size of its previous New York City location. The new office, headed by Tom Lang, General Manager and Vice President, East Coast Sales, and Steve Sloane, Northeast Regional Sales Manager, will primarily serve post and broadcast markets throughout the eastern United States and Canada.

The office also includes technical support and business development staff for the local market. "The opening of our new office in this critical location serves as the nucleus for our broadened Americas operation," said Lang. "My mission is to create greater awareness of Snell & Wilcox products in the Eastern region. By increasing our sales and support staff in New York City where it is vital to have a strong presence, we have taken a large step toward accomplishing that goal.

"The new location puts Snell & Wilcox right in the heart of the New York City post market," continued Lang. "It's clear that our new location will have a very positive effect in strengthening Snell & Wilcox's position throughout the Eastern region."
