The INTERNATIONAL ACADEMY OF BROADCASTING (IAB) is currently enrolling students for the next academic year 1 October 2001 - 30 June 2002.

IAB located in Montreux, Switzerland, offers an intensive 9 month postgraduate course in the arts and sciences of radio and television - the language of the Academy is English.

IAB is an independent institution of higher education. Students from all over the world, of different university backgrounds (liberal arts to science and technology) learn the essential aspects of radio and television, carry out numerous practical projects and, through organised or spontaneous discussions, improve their knowledge of the manifold forms of broadcasting.

Presented by prominent personalities from the broadcasting world and university circles, IAB offers the broad and specialised knowledge essential for the new leaders required by the industry and offers the same high level education as the best MBA schools in their areas.

Details of the IAB programme, terms and conditions, list of faculty members and application form, are available on website or can be sent on request by paper mail.
