Since 1985

The leadership of the HDTV movement has been served by Advanced Television Publishing (ATP) for nearly 18 years.

For twelve years ATP authored the industry bible--the HDTV Newsletter.

For four of those years (in association with Mecklermedia) the company assembled and edited the world's only journal on the subject--HDTV World Review.

ATP publisher (Dale Cripps) recently celebrated his ninth year as the HDTV technical editor for the prestigious magazine, Widescreen Review.

In addition Mr. Cripps has authored 80 articles on HDTV for numerous professional and consumer publications.

A long string of international reports on the subject have also been prepared and filed by the company on behalf of their European, Japanese, and Taiwanese clients.

Four industry-wide International conferences were hosted and programmed by ATP during the formative1990s.

HDTV MAGAZINE
In November of 1998 ATP launched the world's first consumer publication focused entirely upon the DTV transition--HDTV Magazine (officially endorsed by CEA).

VOICE OF EXPERIENCE FOR YOU
A long standing network of professional colleagues and alliances insures us of having a unique "voice" within the industry. This voice-of-authority is now available to retailers, broadcasters, program providers, and consumer groups alike.

As time and money permit we, add to our DTV "reference" web site. This site is designed to present in clear and understandable terms an encylopedia of fundamentals and sophistications of digital television. We are making it easier for our clients to educate their consumers by providing a definitive and authoritative web resource produced by one of the more-recognized independent observers and activists in the field.

Clearing up confusion while delivering a clear understanding and eager message of acceptance for HDTV is the chief objective in our work. We believe that HDTV makes a positive contribution to the world and it is threatened. For that last reason we see it deserving and requiring a special focus, attention, and handling.
