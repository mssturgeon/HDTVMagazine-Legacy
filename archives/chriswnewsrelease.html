Definition Productions Announces 24p High Definition Camera Services

Definition Productions Announces 24p High Definition Camera Services

CHRIS WISSINGER

Specializing in HIGH DEFINITION Production

Owner/ Operator Sony CineAlta 24p HDW-F900 & Sony 1080i HDW-700A

HD Cinematographer, HD Camera Operator, Digital Camera Assistant

Los Angeles, CA / Orlando, FL – February 28, 2001 - Chris Wissinger of Definition Productions (CA, FL) announced today the acquisition of the new Sony CineAlta 24p Sony HDW-F900 multi-recording format HIGH DEFINITON Camera.

Chris is an owner of the new Sony CineAlta 24p HDW-F900 HD camera and has been an owner of the Sony 1080i HDW-700A HD Camera for the past two years. Definition Productions specializes in High Definition Camera Support Services in both Los Angeles, CA and Orlando, FL. Chris Wissinger has been working exclusively with High Definition on over 75 projects, both locally and internationally as a HD Cinematographer, HD Camera Operator, & Digital Camera Assistant for the past four years. Chris also performs duties a HD Production Consultant and on-set engineer. Chris is the first production company in the state of Florida to offer 24p High Definition Production Services. If you are interested in High Definition Production, CineAlta 24p, or about Chris’s commitment to HD Production for Features, Commercials, Episodic, Industrial, Corporate and Multi-Media projects, please contact Chris.
