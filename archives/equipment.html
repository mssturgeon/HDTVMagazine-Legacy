We See Three Sequential Levels Of Upgrade To Handle Digital Broadcasts.

A. Network Pass-Thru
New digital (HDTV) network feeds are stored on digital VCRs (D2, D3) and/or video servers at 19Mbps. Delayed broadcast is then run through new digital transmitter. Minimum investment in transmitter, antenna, downlink, 19Mbps capable storage devices.

B. Upconvert local NTSC
Locally produced NTSC is upconverted to digital (HDTV) and MPEG2 encoded to be mixed with network feed. Medium investment in all of Network Pass equipment and HDTV upconverter, MPEG2/transport encoder.

C. Local HDTV production
Produce local news/weather/sports/commercials/programs in HDTV. Upgrade entire studio and production room to handle either uncompressed or mildly compressed HDTV. Maximum investment in Network Pass equipment, Upconvert local NTSC equipment and all new cameras, production/on-air switcher, DVE, Graphics, HDTV capable storage devices, etc.
