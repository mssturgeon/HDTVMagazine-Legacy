About The Publisher...

Carrying The Torch for HDTV While the transition to digital TV crawls forward, there are folks out there who have literally invested a lifetime into bringing the creme de la creme of DTV -- high definition television, or HDTV -- from a dream to reality. Dale Cripps of Alsea, Oregon, has been publishing and editing a variety of HDTV newsletters and online daily HDTV program guides since the early 1980s, when he first saw and was blown over by HDTV. Dale's opinions and commentaries on HDTV have been quoted extensively over the years by the mainstream press, and he has also conducted many thought-provoking interviews with industry leaders from the FCC, CBS, ABC, CEA, NCTA, and NAB, as well as equipment manufacturers, analysts, and other notables.

I can't think of anyone else who has lobbied as long and hard for a widespread adoption of an all-HDTV system, nor can I recall anyone who has put in the long hours and invested so many dollars into spreading the word and reaching out to HDTV newbies and enthusiasts.

Dale's daily email program guide and the thought-provoking periodic "Page 2" commentary/interview/product review section are available for a very reasonable subscription through his web site at www.ilovehdtv.com. There's even a HDTV Tips Forum where subscribers can exchange information and provide technical assistance. If you are looking for an excellent HDTV resource, check out this site and sign yourself up."

Pete Putman, Primedia
(Noted author and HDTV authority)
