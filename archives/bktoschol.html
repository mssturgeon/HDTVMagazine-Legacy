UCLA EXTENSION

CONSUMER ELECTRONICS SHORT COURSE

 

 

This winter and spring, UCLA Extension will present the following consumer electronics short courses on the UCLA campus in Los Angeles.

February 23-25, 2000, "Error Control Coding: Algorithms and Implementation". The instructors are Richard Wesel, PhD, Assistant Professor, Electrical Engineering Department, UCLA; and Alan atherer, PhD, Senior Member of the Technical Staff and Manager, Wireless Communications Group, Texas Instruments, $1195.

February 28-March 3, 2000, "Spoken Language Dialog Systems". The instructors are Shrikanth Narayanan, PhD, Senior Member of the Technical Staff, and Roberto Pieraccini, Dr. Ing, Principal Member of he Technical Staff, AT&T Labs-Research, $1495.

March 6-10, 2000, "Turbo Codes: Analysis, Design, Performance, and Implementation". The instructors are Sergio Benedetto, PhD, rofessor, Polytecnico di Torino; Dariush Divsalar, PhD, Senior Member of the Technical Staff, Jet Propulsion Laboratory; Guido Montorsi, PhD, Postdoctoral Fellow, Polytecnico di Torino; and Fabrizio Pollara, PhD, Technical Group Supervisor, Jet Propulsion Laboratory, $1795.

March 27-28, 2000, "Wireless Data Systems". The instructor is Peter Rysavy, MSEE, consultant, $895. March 29-31, 2000, "Digital Cellular Systems". The instructor is C. R. "Rick" Baugh, PhD, consultant, $1295 (combined price, $1695).

April 24-25, 2000, "Introduction to Phase-Locked Loops". The instructor is Donald R. Stephens, PhD, President and Chief Scientist, CommLargo, Inc., $795.

April 26-28, 2000, "Design of Wireless Modems". The instructor is Donald R. Stephens, PhD, President and Chief Scientist, CommLargo, Inc., $1195.

May 8-12, 2000, "Charge-Coupled Devices/CMOS Imaging Sensors and Cameras". The instructors are James R. Janesick, MS, PixelVision; Terrence Lomheim, PhD, The Aerospace Corporation; and Bedabrata Pain, PhD, Jet Propulsion Laboratory, $1695.

May 10-12, 2000, "Compression for Digital TV". The instructors are Wayne McLachlan and Peter Symes, Technology Managers, Grass Valley Products, Tektronix, Inc., $1295.

May 15-19, 2000, "Digital Signal Processing: Theory, Algorithms, and Implementation". The instructor is Robert W. Stewart, PhD, Faculty Member, Department of Electronic and Electrical Engineering, University of Strathclyde, United Kingdom, $1695.
