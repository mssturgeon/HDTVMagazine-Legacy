Dear Colleagues,

Over the past couple of years, the Internet has proved to be the fastest growing area in the domain of communications, embracing a huge variety of applications from person to person communication, through advertising, information dissemination and distance learning to banking and business transactions.

Is the Internet a threat or a golden opportunity for broadcasters? Is the Internet only a means of publicising broadcasters' programmes, or much more than that? How can the Internet be delivered via broadcasting channels and how can "traditional" broadcasting contents be delivered via the Internet? Will Internet delivery change viewing and listening habits? Will the Internet and broadcasting convergence bring forward a new form of global media?

All these and many other questions will be debated and investigated during the INTERNET BROADCASTING SEMINAR organised by the International Academy of Broadcasting. Do not miss this opportunity to receive from the most prominent specialists information about the technology which will dramatically change your business in the years to come !
