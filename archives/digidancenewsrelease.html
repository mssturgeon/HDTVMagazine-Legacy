Thursday, January 18, 2001

Digidance

Sunday January 21 is HD day at Digidance, held at the same time as Sundance in Park City. Three features will be shown: "My Sister's Wedding" at 10AM, "Swimming Upstream" at 12 noon, and "Nicolas" (the revised edit) at 2:30. "Swimming Upstream" and "Nicolas" were both shot in 24P and onlined at APS in Los Angeles. At 4:00PM there will be a panel on "The Current and Future State of HD." More information about Digidance can be found at Digidanceonline.com.
