Broadcasters Want On The Information Highway

The broadcast industry relies heavily upon a digital data future, seeing in it a way of defraying cost associated with establishing digital high-definition service. But coming litererally over their shoulders is a new breed of low orbit satellites and data services from high powered geostationary satellites, such as the Dish Network and Hughes' DirecTV PC Connection. The role of broadcaster on the digital information highway is in danger of changing--losing out to competitive forces--should political and market conditions keep broadcasting from moving forward soon.

Dale Cripps

October, 1996
