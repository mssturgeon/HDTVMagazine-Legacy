The FCC decided upon the DTV standard December 24th, 1996. Their principal involvement ended with the granting (at no cost) of the digital spectrum to broadcasters. To insure a transition the FCC did set deadlines causing full national digital broadcast services within 9 years, and the top markets within just 24 months. Neither Cable nor DBS is forced to carry H/DTV signals. With these last government decisions the DTV/HDTV movement was set free for commercialization.
