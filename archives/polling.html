Polling Booth Results

 

Not altogether scientific, but the responses to our web polling do represent the opinions of some people--those same people who are customers and future customers of HDTV products.

 

 

    Do you understand the value of digital television?

    Response:

    Yes 81.5% | No 18.5%

    The Second Question: Have you seen an HDTV broadcast?

    Response:

    Yes 68.5% | No 31.5%

    Would you pay a subscription price of $100 per month for first run movies
    delivered to your HDTV via satellite?

    Response:

    Yes 47.4% | No 52.6%

    Do you plan on purchasing a HDTV set in 1999?

    Response:

    Yes 76.1% | No 23.9%

    Which network has done the best job introducing HDTV to the public to date?

    Response:

    CBS 50.8% | ABC 49.2%

    Is HDTV Worth the Current Retail Price?

    Response:

    Yes 73.3% | No 26.7%

    Have You Heard of the NAB?

    Response:

    Yes 47.8% | No 52.2%

    Will the upcoming nightly broadcasts of "The Tonight Show with Jay Leno"
    in HDTV be the spark that fuels the HDTV explosion?

    Response:

    Yes 41.9% | No 58.1%

    Which HDTV format do you prefer?

    Response:

    1080i 75.6% | 720p 24.4%

    Have you seen "The Tonight Show with Jay Leno" in HDTV

    Response:

    Yes 29.7% | No 70.1%

    Have you seen 1080i and 720p displayed side by side?

    Response:

    Yes 55.8% | No 44.2%

    Are you anticipating Monday Night Football in HDTV on ABC?

    Response:

    Yes 29% | No 71%

    To Date, Has HDTV Been Properly Introduced to the American Public?

    Response:

    Yes 23.1% | No 76.9%

    Have you heard of Konka?

    Response:

    Yes 26.5% | No 73.5%

    Which is more important in your decision to purchase an HDTV?

    Response:

    Price 28.5% | Programming 71.5%

    Have you shopped for an HDTV?

    Response:

    Yes 34.3% | No 65.7%
