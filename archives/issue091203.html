September 12, 2003

The following statement was issued this morning by Touchstone Television on the death of actor John Ritter:


"On behalf of the Touchstone Television family, we are deeply saddened by the tragic death of John Ritter. To all who knew him he was an inspiration. His endless talents and unparalleled human spirit were a gift to us all that we will cherish. While he had the uncommon gift of making everyone he came in contact with feel special, he was the truly special one. Our thoughts and prayers are with his family. He will be forever remembered and forever missed."

-- Stephen McPherson, President, Touchstone Television
