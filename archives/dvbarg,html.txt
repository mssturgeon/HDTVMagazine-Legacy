The Argentineans hadreportedly wanted to hold the seminar earlier but the IBC conventiontends to tie up most of the DVB's upper echelons.<br>							<br>							&quot;They now feel that they require more information about what isactually going on. So they have called for a conference at this pointin time to discuss these issues,&quot; explains Anthony Smith, the DVB'shead of marketing. The DVB Project will provide technical expertisefrom the UK, Australia, Singapore and  even India to discuss theircountries' respective DVB-T roll out plans. But after making theircase in Argentina, all eyes will be on Brazil says MacAvock, &quot;My viewis that Brazil is the key. If Brazil goes DVB then the rest of SouthAmerica will follow,&quot; he says.<br>							<br>							Brazil is currently conducting comparative experiments between thetwo systems.
